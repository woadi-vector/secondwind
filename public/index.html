Second Wind

Walking music from the songs they already love

Name a few favorite artists and set the pace of their steps. Second Wind finds music they are likely to love, checks the beat of every song, and keeps only the ones that are steady enough to walk to.

Tell us about the class

In your own words: what music they love, who has a favorite, how you like to run it. Claude reads it and fills in the settings below, and you can change anything.

Who do they love to listen to?

Add one to four artists. Different styles work well together.

How fast do they walk?

Tap the pad once with each step while they walk, or use the buttons. Most people walk between 90 and 115 steps a minute; a physical therapist can set a target.
